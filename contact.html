Questions we are often asked

How do I book?

Use the booking page. Pick a facility or an event, choose a date and time, and check the price. You get a reference number to bring to the front desk. You can also ring us.

Do I pay online?

No. You pay at the front desk when you arrive, by card or cash. There are no booking fees.

Can I cancel or change a booking?

Yes. Hire bookings can be cancelled for free up to 48 hours before. Event tickets can be refunded up to 7 days before the event. You can cancel online under My bookings. The details are in our terms and conditions.

What happens if the weather is bad?

Outdoor tennis courts and the 3G pitches close in ice, standing water or lightning. If we close a facility on the day, you can pick another slot or get a full refund.

What should I wear?

Clean, non-marking indoor shoes in the sports hall, moulded studs or trainers on the 3G pitches, and a swimming costume or trunks in the pool. Hats and goggles are on sale at the front desk.

Can children come on their own?

Children under 16 need an adult with them, unless they are part of a coached club session. Under-8s must always be with an adult in the pool.

How do I join as a member?

Ask at the front desk. Members get 15% off hire and early access to event tickets. See the membership prices.
