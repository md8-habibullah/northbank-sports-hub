Privacy

What we collect, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

Sample text. This page was written for a demonstration website. Northbank Sports Hub is a fictional centre and this site does not collect real personal data.

Last updated 15 November 2026.

What we collect

When you book or contact us, we ask for your name and email address. You can also give a phone number and notes, such as access needs. For memberships we also ask for proof of eligibility, such as a student ID.

Why we use it

  • To make and manage your booking, and to contact you about it.
  • To keep you safe, for example by planning for access needs.
  • To meet our legal duties, such as keeping accounts.

Cookies and browser storage

This website does not use cookies, advertising or tracking. When you use the booking form, the page keeps your booking in your browser's session storage so the summary page can show it. It is removed when you close the tab. In this demonstration, nothing is sent to a server.

Who sees your information

Only our own staff. We do not sell your information or share it for marketing.

How long we keep it

We keep booking records for 12 months, and membership records while you are a member and for 12 months after. Accident and safeguarding records are kept for as long as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask to see what we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted. You can also object to how we use it. Email hello@northbank-hub.example and we will reply within one month. If you are unhappy with our answer, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Contact

Northbank Sports Hub, 12 Riverside Walk, Northbank, NB1 4AB. Phone 020 7946 0123. See also our terms and conditions.
