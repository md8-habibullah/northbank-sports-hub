Where you can play

See all facilities

  • 5-a-side pitches

    Two floodlit 3G pitches with rebound boards, for matches and training.

    From £38 per pitch, per hour

  • Sports hall: basketball

    A full-size indoor court with a sprung floor and adjustable hoops.

    From £30 per court, per hour

  • Sports hall: badminton

    Six marked courts with posts and nets. Book a single court or a whole row.

    From £10 per court, per hour

  • Floodlit tennis courts

    Four acrylic hard courts, floodlit so you can play after work.

    From £14 per court, per hour

  • 25 m swimming pool

    Six lanes, hire them by the lane for club training or a private swim.

    From £8 per lane, per hour

  • 400 m running track

    A six-lane synthetic track around a grass infield. Hire it for a club, school or run group.

    From £20 per group, per hour

Clear prices, no booking fees

You pay by the hour. Weekday daytimes are cheaper, and evenings from 17:00 and weekends are peak. Members get 15% off hire.

How booking works

  1. Choose a facility or an event, and a date and time.
  2. Check the price, which updates as you choose.
  3. Confirm your booking and keep the reference number. You can see or cancel it any time under My bookings.
  4. Turn up and pay at the front desk. Nothing is charged online.
