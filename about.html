About us

We are a community sports centre that wants more people playing, whatever their level.

Our story

Northbank Sports Hub started as a petition. In 2009 a group of local parents, swimmers and five-a-side regulars asked the council for one place where everyone in the area could play, whatever their age, budget or experience.

The Hub opened in 2012 with a pool and a sports hall, and has grown with the people who use it. Today it is run as a community organisation, and any money left after costs goes back into lower prices, coaching and equipment.

We keep the aim simple: make it easy to find a space, easy to see what it costs, and easy to turn up and play.

How we got here

  1. 2009

    Local residents petition for a shared sports centre.

  2. 2012

    The Hub opens with a 25 m pool and a sports hall.

  3. 2016

    Two floodlit 3G pitches are added beside the river.

  4. 2020

    Four floodlit tennis courts and the running track open.

  5. 2024

    A solar roof now covers the sports hall's energy use during the day.

  6. 2026

    The new online booking system and events programme launch.

What we stand for

Open to everyone
Prices are on the page, concessions are real, and beginners are as welcome as club players.
Safe to play
Trained staff and lifeguards are on site, equipment is checked every day, and under-16s are looked after.
Local first
We hire from the neighbourhood, host local clubs and schools, and give event money to local causes.

Access and support

The whole site is step-free, with accessible changing rooms, a pool hoist, a hearing loop at the front desk and a quiet hour on Tuesday mornings from 10:00 to 11:00. If something would make your visit easier, tell us.

Come and play

The best way to see the Hub is to visit it. Our open day on 28 November has free taster sessions in every sport.
