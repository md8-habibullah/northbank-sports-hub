Booking prices

Prices are per hour for hire, with cheaper off-peak times. No booking fees.

Facility hire

Hire is charged by the hour. A booking that crosses 17:00 on a weekday is charged at the off-peak rate up to 17:00 and the peak rate after.

When is peak?

  • Off-peak: Monday to Friday, before 17:00.
  • Peak: Monday to Friday from 17:00, and all day Saturday and Sunday.

Example: a 5-a-side pitch on a Wednesday from 16:00 to 18:00 is one off-peak hour (£38) and one peak hour (£52), so £90. With a membership card, that comes to £76.50.
