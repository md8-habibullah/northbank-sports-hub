Before you go

Tickets and entries

Choose an event on the booking page to see your price. You pay at the entrance on the day. Tickets can be refunded up to 7 days before an event. The full rules are in our terms and conditions.

Getting in and around

The whole site has step-free access, a hearing loop at the front desk and accessible changing rooms. If you need anything arranged in advance, say so in the notes box when you book.
