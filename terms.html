About these terms

Northbank Sports Hub (“the Hub”, “we”, “us”) runs the sports facilities at 12 Riverside Walk, Northbank, NB1 4AB. By booking a facility, buying a ticket or using the Hub, you agree to these terms.

Making a booking

  • You can book on our website, by phone or at the front desk.
  • A booking is confirmed when you receive a reference number.
  • You must be 16 or over to make a booking. Give us accurate contact details so we can reach you if something changes.
  • We may refuse or cancel a booking if the details are wrong or the facility is needed for safety reasons.

Prices and payment

  • The price shown when you book is the price you pay. Our current prices are on the prices page.
  • Peak rates apply from 17:00 on weekdays and all day at weekends. Off-peak rates apply on weekday daytimes before 17:00.
  • There are no booking fees. You pay at the front desk when you arrive, by card or cash.
  • Members must show a valid membership card to get the member discount.

Changing or cancelling a booking

  • You can change or cancel a hire booking for free up to 48 hours before it starts.
  • If you cancel within 48 hours, or do not turn up, you pay the full price unless we can hire the slot to someone else.
  • If you arrive late, your booking still finishes at the booked time.

If we change or cancel

Sometimes we have to close a facility because of ice, flooding, lightning, an equipment fault or an emergency. If that happens, we will contact you, offer another slot, and refund you in full if you prefer. We are not responsible for your travel or other costs.

Event tickets and entries

  • Tickets and entry fees can be refunded up to 7 days before the event. After that, they can only be refunded if we cancel the event or change its date.
  • Free tickets reserve a place. If you cannot come, let us know so someone else can use it.
  • Under-12s do not pay, but need a ticket. Under-16s must be with an adult.
  • The person who enters a team or pair is responsible for the team following the event rules.
  • Times may change on the day. Please follow the instructions of stewards and staff.
  • Outdoor events, such as the tennis championships, go ahead if conditions are safe. If a match cannot be played, the organisers will reschedule it.

Using the facilities

  • Wear suitable footwear: clean, non-marking indoor shoes in the sports hall, and moulded studs or trainers on the 3G pitches.
  • Only water may be taken onto courts and the poolside. Smoking and vaping are not allowed anywhere on site.
  • Follow staff instructions and the posted safety rules, especially in the pool.
  • Children under 16 need an adult with them, unless they are in a coached club session. Under-8s must be with an adult in the pool.
  • Please report any injury or damaged equipment to staff straight away.

Membership

  • Membership is paid monthly and renews until you cancel. Cancel by giving us 14 days' notice before the next payment.
  • The member discount is 15% off facility hire. It does not apply to equipment packs or event tickets.
  • Membership is personal. The Family plan covers 2 adults and up to 3 children under 16 in the same household.
  • Student and over-60s plans need valid ID.

Our responsibility

We take reasonable care of everyone who uses the Hub. We are not responsible for loss of or damage to your belongings, so please use the lockers. Nothing in these terms limits our responsibility for death or personal injury caused by our negligence, or anything else the law does not allow us to limit. You are responsible for damage you cause beyond normal wear and tear.

Photos and filming

We may take photos at events to show what the Hub offers. Tell a member of staff if you do not want yourself or your child photographed. Filming is not allowed in changing areas or the pool.

Changes to these terms

We may update these terms from time to time. The version on this page when you make a booking is the one that applies to that booking.
