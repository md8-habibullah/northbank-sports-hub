Good to know

What is in the equipment pack?

For £6 per booking you get what suits your facility: balls and bibs for the pitches, rackets and shuttles or balls for the courts, cones and stopwatches for the track. Add it when you book.

Can I hire a facility for a birthday or a school group?

Yes. Book the facility for the time you need and add the function room if you want food afterwards. For groups of more than 30, call us so we can plan staffing.

Are the facilities accessible?

The whole site is step-free. There are accessible changing rooms, a pool hoist, a hearing loop at the front desk and four accessible parking spaces. Tell us what you need in the notes when you book.

Where do I park?

There are 40 spaces at the Hub, including 4 accessible ones, and bike racks by the front door. Northbank station is a five-minute walk.
